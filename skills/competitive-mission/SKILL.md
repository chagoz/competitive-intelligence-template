# Competitive Mission

## Status and cadence

**Mode:** ad hoc, on demand, always a live conversation. Never a candidate for scheduling, by definition the request is not known in advance.

## The defining rule

**If the request is vague, ask for scope clarification before researching. Never guess at what "good" looks like for an undefined ask.**

**If comparative analysis against your own company is requested and the necessary input on your side isn't available, flag the gap explicitly and ask whether to proceed without it or wait. Never fabricate your own side of a comparison.**

## Before anything else

Read from Project knowledge, scoped to what the mission actually needs, not all of it by default: `about-us.md`, `analysis-rules.md` (apply the same source honesty and recency rules as every other output, a mission is not exempt).

For whatever competitors the mission touches, search the Competitor Profiles database (title confirmed in `configuration.md`) for relevant entries. If `configuration.md` doesn't have this confirmed yet, search Notion by name, confirm, and write it back.

## Output

A standalone Notion page under the Competitive Intelligence home page (title confirmed in `configuration.md`), structure determined by the mission itself, not a fixed template. Apply `language-guidelines.md` Part 1 (system-wide: source honesty, recency, no em dashes, visual legends) regardless of structure. Whether Part 2 or Part 3 voice fits better depends on the intended reader, ask if it's not obvious from the request.

**Precedents worth reading before starting, for pattern reference, not to copy verbatim:** search the Competitive Intelligence home page for any past mission pages that exist in this workspace, prior missions are often the fastest way to see what a good structure looks like for a similar question.

## What this skill does not do

Does not assume a request is well-scoped just because it sounds specific. Does not invent your own side of a comparison to fill a gap. Does not run on a schedule.
