# Slack Message Template

*How weekly digest findings get distributed to Slack so people actually read them.*

*Last updated: August 13, 2026*

---

## Fixed structure

```
📡 Weekly Competitor Digest #[N] · [Date]

1. [Takeaway]
2. [Takeaway]
3. [Takeaway]

Read more → [link to the full digest]

[Closing line]
```

The title, three-point structure, and link are always identical in shape. The closing line is the only variable element.

## Bullet formula

Each bullet = what they did + what it means for us, in one sentence. Second part maximum four words.

Good: "[Competitor A] claimed our AI positioning. They published first. We haven't."
Good: "[Competitor B] is now a data layer, not just a rating. Different game entirely."
Bad: "[Competitor B] published new methodology documentation this week." (fact only, no implication, this is what drives low engagement)

## Closing line, contextual, not formulaic

Before writing the closing line, always ask: "Anything specific from this week I should connect to, office mood, weather, a running joke? If not, I'll work from the week's signals alone."

Three registers to rotate between, chosen based on what fits the week, never used as a fixed weekly rotation formula:

**Cult quote / historical enemy framing**, timeless, slightly dramatic.
**Fake kung fu master / monk wisdom**, soft, life-lesson energy, connected to the week's sharpest signal.
**Absurdist**, low-key chaotic, very human.

Avoid repeating the same register two weeks in a row, readers notice.

## Copy rules

- No em dashes, anywhere
- Short sentences, fragments are fine
- Lead with the point, no preamble
- Post to your team's dedicated channel

## Analytics, after the post goes live

Track total reactions with emoji breakdown, thread reply count, and Notion view counts at 24 hours and one week. Log these to an impact tracker so engagement patterns become visible over time, not just remembered anecdotally. Low click-through on a strong Slack post is not automatically a failure, if the Slack message rule (`language-guidelines.md`, Part 1) is followed correctly, the post delivers the core message on its own, so a reader with thirty seconds already got the point without needing to click through.
