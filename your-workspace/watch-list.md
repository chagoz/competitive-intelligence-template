# Watch List

*The topic areas you actively monitor. Not a news feed, a defined set of questions the system asks every cycle: did anything move on the things we actually care about?*

---

## Why a watch list matters

Without one, research scans broadly and misses depth. With one, every digest and reality check answers the same specific questions instead of reacting to whatever surfaced that week. The watch list is also what makes scoring possible, a competitor move only scores as high-threat if it strikes one of these topics.

## How to choose yours

Limit to two to four topics. More creates noise, everything starts scoring as urgent and the signal disappears. For each topic, ask:

- What is the primary competitive battleground in your category right now?
- What external forces (regulation, technology, market shifts) most affect your buyers, regardless of what any single competitor does?

A good topic is specific enough to score signals against. "Innovation" is too broad to ever be false. "Whether competitors ship auditable AI explainability features" is specific enough that you can say yes or no about a given week.
