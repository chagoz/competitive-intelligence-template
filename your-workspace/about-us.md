## What else could sharpen this document

*Pick whichever of these apply to you, none are mandatory, but each one makes competitor scoring measurably sharper:*

- **Pricing tiers or ranges**, even rough positioning (budget, mid-market, premium), sharpens how threatening a competitor's price move actually is
- **Named recent wins and losses**, real deal outcomes are the sharpest fuel for a specific "so what for us," pull from your CRM if you have one
- **Product roadmap direction**, even directional, so a competitive gap reads as closing soon or staying open
- **Industry or vertical specifics**, if you sell differently to different sectors, note it here, it changes how a competitor's vertical move should score
- **RFI or RFP answer bank**, common questions you're asked and your standard answers, useful raw material for reality-check reasoning
- **Who reads your outputs and what they do with them**, mapped to actual decisions, sharpens who a finding is implicitly written for
- **An honest self-assessment of where you're genuinely behind**, not just where competitors exaggerate, the sharpest insights usually come from here
