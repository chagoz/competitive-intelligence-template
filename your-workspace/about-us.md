## What else could sharpen this document

*None of these are mandatory, but each makes competitor scoring measurably sharper:*

- **Pricing tiers or ranges**, even rough positioning
- **Named recent wins and losses**, real deal outcomes, pulled from your CRM
- **Product roadmap direction**, even directional
- **Industry or vertical specifics**, if you sell differently across sectors
- **RFI or RFP answer bank**, common questions and your standard answers
- **Who reads your outputs and what they do with them**, mapped to actual decisions
- **An honest self-assessment of where you're genuinely behind**, not just where competitors exaggerate
